## Coverage definition

- Entries are grouped by package, owning type, and name; overloads share a page. One sample does not imply that every overload was verified.
- Public and protected declarations are included. Private, internal, HIDDEN, and legacy DOM compatibility sources excluded by the stdlib build are omitted.
- An entry has examples only if at least one representative program really compiled and ran. Every platform declaration retains a pinned source link.
- Chinese KDoc is a machine-translated draft; English retains the official prose. All missing examples are listed in the repository's `data/coverage.json`.
